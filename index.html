About Chiya Mantra

Come for the tea. Stay for the vibe.

Welcome to Chiya Mantra—a cozy destination where flavorful food, refreshing drinks, and a peaceful atmosphere come together to create the perfect place to relax, connect, and enjoy good moments.

At Chiya Mantra, tea is at the heart of what we do. We offer a variety of teas to suit every taste, with our signature Matka Tea taking center stage. Alongside our tea selection, we serve a range of flavorful food and refreshing beverages, prepared with quality ingredients and a passion for great taste. We proudly serve fresh Momo, Noodles, Fried Rice, Biryani, and an assorted menu of Veg & Non-Veg snacks.

Our café is designed to offer a peaceful and cozy atmosphere, surrounded by lush plants, warm low lighting, and a relaxed ambience. It’s the perfect setting for friends and families to hang out, catch up, and create memorable moments.

For those who enjoy a laid-back experience, we also offer Hookah and a selection of indoor board games (including UNO, Jenga Block, and Ludo) to keep you entertained while waiting or simply enjoying time with your company.

And when you’d rather enjoy Chiya Mantra from the comfort of your home, we’ve got you covered with our late-night home delivery service. Whether it’s a late-night craving, a cozy evening at home, or simply your favorite food and tea, you can enjoy the Chiya Mantra experience wherever you are.

At Chiya Mantra, we believe a great café experience is about more than food and drinks. It’s about good tea, flavorful food, great company, a welcoming atmosphere, and moments worth remembering.

Come for the tea. Stay for the vibe. Enjoy Chiya Mantra anytime, anywhere.
